# Decisions

Why kaloli-bot is built the way it is. Newest last.

## 2026-11-18: first version

**Goal.** A group of friends posts meal photos to a Telegram group. A bot estimates calories with the cheapest vision model that does the job, includes any caption as context, stores everything, and reports per person at the end of the day. It runs on one always-on Mac.

**Long polling, not a webhook.** The bot calls Telegram; nothing calls the bot. No public URL, tunnel or open port on a home machine.

**Bun with no dependencies.** `bun:sqlite` and `fetch` cover the whole job. A Telegram framework would be larger than the bot.

**SQLite, one file.** A handful of people and a few photos a day. Backups are a file copy.

**Any OpenAI-compatible vision endpoint.** The bot only needs `POST /v1/chat/completions` with image input, so the model and provider are configuration.

**Model: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.** Three low-cost Flash models were tried on the same meal photo through the same endpoint. All answered in 2 to 2.5 seconds with similar breakdowns (489 to 550 kcal); the newest lite model was kept as the default. With the note "ate half" its estimate dropped from 500 to 269 kcal, and a screenshot with no food was rejected.

**Tag to trigger.** The default is to analyse only photos that tag the bot. A friends' group also carries photos that are not food, and each analysis spends model quota. `TRIGGER_MODE=all` exists for groups used only for meals.

**Allowlist, failing closed.** Anyone can add a public bot to their own group. Only allowlisted chats reach the model, and an empty list means none.

**Privacy mode disabled.** Telegram's documentation does not state that a tagged photo caption reaches a bot in privacy mode. Disabling it removes the doubt; the bot still discards everything it was not addressed in.

**The total is the sum of the items.** Models sometimes return a `total_kcal` that disagrees with their own items. Showing a breakdown that does not add up would undermine every number, so the items win.

**A meal belongs to the photo's sender and day.** Someone else may tag a forgotten photo hours later; the calories are still the sender's, on the day the photo was posted.

**One entry per photo.** Tagging again with a better note replaces the estimate. Corrections must not inflate totals.

**Report at 23:55 with catch-up.** A laptop may be asleep at 23:55. Reports are tracked per chat and day and sent late rather than lost, up to 3 days back.

**`/rapor` and `/sil` were added unasked.** A wrong entry would otherwise sit in the totals until someone edited the database.

## 2026-11-19: leaderboard

`/siralama` ranks people by total calories for the current Monday-based week, with `bugun`, `ay` and `hepsi` variants. It shows the daily average once a person has entries on more than one day. Ordering is highest total first; whether "most" or "least" wins is the group's call, and the order is one line in `Store.leaderboard`.

## 2026-11-19: piece counts and the assumption line

A member posted a plate of eggs with the note "there were 5 eggs, I ate 2". The reply read "Haşlanmış yumurta 100 g — 155 kcal" and looked as if one egg had been counted. The stored raw reply showed the model had counted two; the estimate was right and the presentation hid it.

Two changes followed:

- Items carry a `count` for foods eaten by the piece, shown as "2 adet · 100 g".
- The model's one-sentence assumption, already stored, is printed under each reply.

The same photo was re-run read-only with the new prompt: with the note it counted 2 eggs (155 kcal), without the note 3 eggs (215 kcal).

## 2026-11-20: project and publication

Renamed to kaloli-bot. The LaunchAgent plist is generated by `bin/service` at install time, so the repository carries no machine-specific paths.

## Not built

- **Token and quota accounting.** Neither the bot nor the endpoint records usage per request.
- **Manual correction of numbers.** An estimate can be replaced by re-tagging with a note, or deleted; it cannot be edited by hand.
- **Per-person calorie goals** and weekly or monthly scheduled reports.
- **Photos are not archived.** Only Telegram's file id is kept.
